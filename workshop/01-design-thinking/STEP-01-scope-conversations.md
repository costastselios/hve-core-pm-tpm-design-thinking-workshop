# Step 1 — Scope Conversations

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** frame user, problem, outcome, boundaries and unknowns. **HVE’s job:** coach/structure the scope.

## Do
1. Select **DT Coach**.
2. Provide [ambiguous-product-idea.md](../00-start/ambiguous-product-idea.md).
3. Paste:
```text
Use HVE Core Design Thinking to develop this early idea. Treat it as hypotheses, not validated requirements. Start at the appropriate Design Thinking method. Do not jump to BRD, PRD, backlog or implementation.
```
4. Answer as product decision-maker.

## What good looks like
User, problem hypothesis, desired outcome, boundaries and unknowns are explicit.

## Stop
Method 1 exits and DT Coach is ready for Design Research.

## Recover if needed
Ask DT Coach to check Method 1 exit criteria, list what is missing, and advance only if satisfied.
