# Step 1 — Scope Conversations

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** frame user, problem, outcome, boundaries and unknowns. **HVE’s job:** coach and structure scope. **Do not worry about:** BRD, PRD, backlog or implementation.

## Do
1. Select **DT Coach**.
2. [Open the ambiguous product idea](../00-start/ambiguous-product-idea.md) and provide it to Copilot Chat.
3. Paste:
```text
Use HVE Core Design Thinking to develop this early idea. Treat it as hypotheses, not validated requirements. Start at the appropriate Design Thinking method. Do not jump to BRD, PRD, backlog or implementation.
```
4. Answer as the product decision-maker.

## What good looks like
You can explain the user, problem hypothesis, desired outcome, boundaries, and what still needs evidence.

## Stop
Method 1 exit criteria are met and DT Coach is ready for Design Research.

## Recover if needed
```text
Check Method 1 exit criteria against our artifacts. Tell me what is missing. If satisfied, close Method 1 and initialize Method 2 without changing the evidence.
```
