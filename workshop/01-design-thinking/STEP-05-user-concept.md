# Step 5 — User Concept

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** make product choices and keep uncertainty explicit. **HVE’s job:** structure the concept. **Do not worry about:** architecture or detailed implementation.

## Do
1. Let DT Coach turn the direction into a user-centered concept.
2. Make product decisions yourself when prompted.
3. Keep unresolved implementation/calculation details open.
4. Now compare your concept with the [canonical concept snapshot](../03-concept-checkpoint/canonical-concept-snapshot.md).
5. Use that snapshot as the shared Step 6 baseline.

## What good looks like
The concept explains the experience and trust model while keeping implementation uncertainty open.

## Stop
The concept has a user promise, journey, action hierarchy, trust model, D/F/V assumptions, open decisions, and validation boundary.

## Recover if needed
```text
Review Method 5 for user promise, journey, action hierarchy, trust model, D/F/V assumptions, open decisions, and validation boundary. Add only missing structure and do not invent evidence.
```
