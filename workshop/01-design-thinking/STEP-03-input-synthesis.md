# Step 3 — Input Synthesis

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
Make evidence-aware framing decisions.

## Do
Let DT Coach cluster evidence/themes/problem framing. Preserve participant-specific evidence, contradictions, assumptions and open questions.

## What good looks like
A focused opportunity frame exists without flattening evidence.

## Stop
Method 3 exit criteria are met.

## Recover if needed
Ask DT Coach to evaluate Method 3 exit criteria and list missing content before advancing.
