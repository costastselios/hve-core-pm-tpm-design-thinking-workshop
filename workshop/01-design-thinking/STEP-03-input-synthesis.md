# Step 3 — Input Synthesis

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** make evidence-aware framing judgments. **HVE’s job:** cluster and synthesize evidence. **Do not worry about:** eliminating contradictions.

## Do
1. Let DT Coach create evidence cards/clusters/themes/problem framing.
2. Resolve synthesis tensions as product/research judgments.
3. Keep shared evidence separate from participant-specific evidence.

## What good looks like
You have a focused opportunity frame without flattening contradictions or participant-specific evidence.

## Stop
Shared signals, participant-specific findings, tensions, assumptions, open questions, and a focused opportunity frame exist.

## Recover if needed
```text
Check Method 3 exit criteria. Preserve contradictions and confidence limits. If met, close Method 3 and initialize Method 4.
```
