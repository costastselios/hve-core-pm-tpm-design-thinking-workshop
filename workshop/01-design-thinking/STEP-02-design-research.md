# Step 2 — Design Research

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** let HVE design the research first, then introduce completed evidence deliberately.

## Do
### Stage A — Design the research before showing the evidence
1. Stay with **DT Coach**.
2. Let DT Coach create the contextual-inquiry/interview approach and protocol.
3. **Do not provide the participant research summaries yet.**

### Stage B — Point DT Coach to the completed research
4. When the research protocol is complete, tell DT Coach:
```text
The Design Research interviews have now been completed.

The workshop provides the resulting participant research in:
- workshop/02-research-evidence/participant-01-research-summary.md
- workshop/02-research-evidence/participant-02-research-summary.md

Before interpreting or synthesizing the research, locate and read both files.
Tell me when you have both participant records available and identify them separately.
Do not synthesize them yet.
```
5. If Copilot cannot locate them automatically, provide both files using your VS Code Copilot file-context mechanism.
6. **Wait until DT Coach confirms that both records are available.**

### Stage C — Authorize interpretation only after access is confirmed
7. Then paste:
```text
Use those two participant records as the completed Method 2 research evidence for this workshop.
Treat them as low-confidence directional evidence.
Preserve what appears across both participants, what appears for only one participant, contradictions or different behaviors, assumptions, and unanswered questions.
Do not infer prevalence from two participants.
Continue the normal HVE Design Thinking workflow.
```

## What good looks like
You experienced the sequence **design research → acquire evidence → confirm evidence access → interpret evidence**.

## Stop
DT Coach has both records, preserves their provenance/confidence, and can advance without claiming market validation.

## Recover if needed
If DT Coach starts interpreting before confirming both files, stop it and repeat Stage B. If it stalls later, ask it to check: shared signals, participant differences, assumptions/open questions, and opportunity framing without prevalence claims.
