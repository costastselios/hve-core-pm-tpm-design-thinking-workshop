# Step 2 — Design Research

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** judge what supplied evidence can and cannot support. **HVE’s job:** structure the research method. **Do not worry about:** prevalence or final design.

## Do
1. Let DT Coach create its research approach/protocol.
2. State that this self-paced case uses supplied completed-research summaries.
3. Provide [Participant 01](../02-research-evidence/participant-01-research-summary.md) and [Participant 02](../02-research-evidence/participant-02-research-summary.md).
4. Paste:
```text
These are workshop-supplied summaries of completed participant research, not transcripts. Use them as low-confidence directional evidence. Preserve differences and do not infer prevalence.
```

## What good looks like
You can explain what the summaries support directionally, what differs, and why confidence is low.

## Stop
HVE identifies shared directional signals, preserves material differences, separates assumptions/open questions, and frames opportunities without claiming validation.

## Recover if needed
```text
Assess Method 2 against these criteria:
1. identify shared directional signals across the supplied summaries;
2. preserve material participant differences;
3. distinguish evidence from assumptions and open questions;
4. frame research-backed opportunities without claiming prevalence or product validation.
List missing evidence separately from what can be concluded directionally. If all four are met, initialize Method 3.
```
