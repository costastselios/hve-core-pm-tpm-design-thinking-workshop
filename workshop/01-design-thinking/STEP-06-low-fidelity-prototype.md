# Step 6 — Low-Fidelity Prototype

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** learn from prototypes and prepare the DT evidence handoff. **HVE’s job:** preserve/structure the learning. **Do not worry about:** requirements, architecture or implementation.

## Do
> **Opening prototypes:** in VS Code, right-click the HTML file → **Reveal in Finder/Explorer** → double-click it in your browser. A local HTML preview is also fine.

1. [Open the test script](../04-prototype/test-script.md).
2. Open `../04-prototype/iteration-01/index.html` in a browser and perform every scripted action.
3. Record your self-test in [prototype-self-test.md](../../workshop-output/prototype-self-test.md).
4. [Read the supplied Iteration 1 participant-test evidence](../04-prototype/iteration-01/test-results.md) and give DT Coach the decisive finding: recovery was mechanically possible but current-vs-history consequence was not understood.
5. Treat this as a concept-comprehension failure.
6. Open `../04-prototype/iteration-02/index.html` in a browser and try **both** recovery branches.
7. [Read the Iteration 2 recovery model](../04-prototype/iteration-02/recovery-model.md).
8. Preserve the validation boundary: Iteration 2 is a redesign hypothesis, not representative-user validated.
9. [Complete the DT → BRD handoff](../05-common/DT-TO-BRD-HANDOFF.md) before switching agents.

## What good looks like
You exercised both prototypes, separated self-test from participant evidence, and prepared a complete DT handoff.

## Stop
You can explain the Iteration 1 failure, Iteration 2 redesign, remaining validation gap, and which evidence BRD Builder receives.

## Recover if needed
If a prototype does not respond, refresh once and verify you opened `index.html` rather than source text. If still broken, record a tooling failure; do not claim interaction evidence. If DT evidence exists only in chat, use Case C in the DT→BRD handoff.
