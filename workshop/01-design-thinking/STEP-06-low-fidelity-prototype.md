# Step 6 — Low-Fidelity Prototype

**Agent:** DT Coach

## Your role in this step
Learn from prototypes and prepare the DT evidence handoff.

## Do
Use the [test script](../04-prototype/test-script.md). Open each `index.html` in a browser (right-click → Reveal in Finder/Explorer → double-click). Run Iteration 1, record notes in [prototype-self-test.md](../../workshop-output/prototype-self-test.md), then read [supplied prior test evidence](../04-prototype/iteration-01/test-results.md). Give DT Coach the recovery-comprehension failure. Run both Iteration 2 branches, then read the [recovery model](../04-prototype/iteration-02/recovery-model.md). Complete the [DT→BRD handoff](../05-common/DT-TO-BRD-HANDOFF.md).

## What good looks like
You separate your self-test from participant evidence and preserve the unvalidated redesign boundary.

## Stop
You can explain failure, redesign, validation gap, and evidence handoff.

## Recover if needed
Refresh once if HTML fails. If still broken, record tooling failure and do not claim interaction evidence.
