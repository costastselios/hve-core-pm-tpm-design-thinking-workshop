# Step 0 — Experience the problem domain

**Agent:** None

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** understand the fragmented experience before trying to frame or solve it.

## Do
1. [Open the problem-domain walkthrough](problem-domain.md).
2. Inspect Broker 1, then Broker 2, then Broker 3 in that order.
3. Answer the reflection questions for yourself.
4. Stop when the question **“How do I keep track of all my holdings in one place?”** feels grounded in the scenario.
5. Only then [open the ambiguous product idea](ambiguous-product-idea.md).

## What good looks like
You can explain why each broker can be useful while none provides one complete cross-platform view.

## Stop
You understand the domain tension without having designed a solution or converted the idea into requirements.

## Recover if needed
If you jump to a solution, return to `problem-domain.md` and answer only what each existing broker view can and cannot tell you.
