# Ambiguous Product Idea

I want to build a wealth portfolio tracker that helps a self-directed investor understand investments held across multiple platforms. The idea includes stocks, ETFs, UCITS-related holdings, and crypto, a consolidated view, historical portfolio value, completed-market valuations, regular crypto valuation points, and the ability to establish holdings and later record activity.

This is an early idea. It intentionally mixes problem statements, solution assumptions, and unresolved details. Do not treat it as validated requirements.
