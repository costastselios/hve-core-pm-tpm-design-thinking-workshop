# Ambiguous Product Idea
I want to build a wealth portfolio tracker for a self-directed investor with investments spread across multiple platforms. The idea includes consolidated holdings, factual history, completed-market valuations, multiple asset types/currencies, and ways to establish and maintain holdings.

This mixes problem clues, solution assumptions, and unresolved details. It is not validated requirements.
