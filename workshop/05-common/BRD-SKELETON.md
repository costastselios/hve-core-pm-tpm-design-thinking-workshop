# BRD Minimum Skeleton
This is a review skeleton, not a completed answer.
- Initiative / problem statement
- Business objective and success conditions
- Accountable decision owner
- In scope / out of scope
- Business requirements with stable IDs (for example BR-001)
- Acceptance criteria / measurable condition per requirement
- Assumptions and exclusions
- Open gates/dependencies using `../../docs/GATE-RECORD.md`
- Evidence/decision traceability
- Approval status

**Reject/repair before approval if:** requirements lack traceability, assumptions are stated as facts, open gates are hidden, acceptance criteria are not testable, or scope contradicts exclusions.
