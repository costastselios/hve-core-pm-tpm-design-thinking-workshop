# Step 7 — BRD

**Agent:** BRD Builder

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** decide whether the business baseline is defensible. **HVE’s job:** structure the BRD from handed-forward evidence. **Do not worry about:** product decomposition, architecture or implementation.

## Do
1. Confirm the [DT → BRD handoff](DT-TO-BRD-HANDOFF.md) is complete.
2. Select **BRD Builder**.
3. Paste:
```text
Create the BRD for the Wealth Portfolio Tracker using the completed Design Thinking evidence baseline. Preserve confidence. Do not restart discovery or promote assumptions/participant-specific preferences without justification. Keep regulatory, provider, licensing, validation and technical questions as open gates. No financial amounts. Follow the normal HVE BRD workflow.
```
4. Use the [Step 7 Review Card](STEP-07-REVIEW-CARD.md) as the single review hub.
5. Make material decisions and record gates as required.
6. Approve explicitly:
```text
I approve this BRD as the workshop business baseline for PRD creation. Open dependencies remain unresolved unless explicitly closed with evidence.
```

## What good looks like
Every business requirement is traceable or decision-backed, testable enough to review, and honest about gates.

## Stop
The BRD and BRD→PRD handoff pass the Step 7 Review Card with no approval blockers.

## Recover if needed
If BRD Builder cannot access evidence, use the fallback prompt in [DT → BRD handoff](DT-TO-BRD-HANDOFF.md). If structure is deficient, ask it to repair only missing governance/traceability without inventing evidence or closing gates.
