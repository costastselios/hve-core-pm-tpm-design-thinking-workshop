# BRD-to-PRD Handoff Minimum Skeleton

This is a review skeleton, not a completed answer.

For each business requirement being handed into product definition, capture:
- **Source BRD requirement ID**
- **Business intent / outcome**
- **Destination product requirement or product decision needed**
- **Acceptance boundary**
- **Inherited exclusions**
- **Inherited gates / dependencies**
- **Unresolved product decision**

## Approval blocker
Do not accept the handoff if a BRD requirement silently disappears, an exclusion is lost, or an unresolved gate is presented as solved.
