# Step 8 — PRD

**Agent:** PRD Builder

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** decide whether the product baseline is precise and testable. **HVE’s job:** structure the PRD. **Do not worry about:** architecture, estimates or delivery planning.

## Do
1. Select **PRD Builder** and provide the approved BRD and BRD→PRD handoff.
2. Open the [Decision Rubric](../../docs/DECISION-RUBRIC.md) for material product choices.
3. Paste:
```text
Create the PRD from the approved BRD and BRD-to-PRD handoff. Design Thinking artifacts may support traceability with confidence preserved. Do not repeat discovery, invent architecture, or resolve open gates by assumption. Preserve exclusions and gates. No financial amounts.
```
4. Review against the [PRD skeleton](PRD-SKELETON.md) and [Artifact Checklist](../../docs/ARTIFACT-CHECKLIST.md).
5. When satisfied, paste:
```text
I approve this PRD as the workshop product baseline for Epic/Feature decomposition.
Open dependencies remain gates and are not resolved by this approval.
```

## What good looks like
Product requirements trace to the BRD, have testable acceptance boundaries, and do not invent architecture.

## Stop
Approved PRD with traceability, exclusions, clear boundaries, and visible gates.

## Recover if needed
```text
Review the PRD against the workshop PRD skeleton. Add only missing product, acceptance, traceability, exclusion and gate structure. Do not invent architecture or evidence.
```
