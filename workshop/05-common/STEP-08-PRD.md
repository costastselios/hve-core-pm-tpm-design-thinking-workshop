# Step 8 — PRD

**Agent:** PRD Builder

## Your role in this step
Decide whether the product baseline is precise/testable.

## Do
Provide approved BRD and handoff. Ask PRD Builder to create the PRD without repeating discovery, inventing architecture, or closing gates by assumption. Review against [PRD skeleton](PRD-SKELETON.md), [Decision Rubric](../../docs/DECISION-RUBRIC.md), and [Artifact Checklist](../../docs/ARTIFACT-CHECKLIST.md). Approve explicitly as product planning baseline for Epic/Feature decomposition, with gates still open.

## What good looks like
PRD traces to BRD, has testable boundaries, exclusions and gates.

## Stop
Approved PRD baseline.

## Recover if needed
Repair missing product/acceptance/traceability/gate structure only.
