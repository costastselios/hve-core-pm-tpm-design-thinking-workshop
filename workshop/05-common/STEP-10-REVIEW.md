# Step 10 — Final Product-Owner Review

**Agent:** None

## Your role in this step
**Your job:** verify traceability, gates, confidence, and workshop boundaries. **Do not worry about:** engineering authorization.

## Do
1. Confirm every Feature traces to PRD.
2. Confirm Epics are outcomes and Features are capabilities.
3. Confirm deferred scope is excluded and conditional candidates retain gates.
4. Confirm gate records contain owner, status, exit evidence, affected requirements, and next decision trigger.
5. Confirm there are no Stories/Tasks, architecture, estimates, sprint sequencing, RPI, code, or financial amounts.
6. Confirm Iteration 2 remains unvalidated with representative users.
7. Create `../../workshop-output/WORKSHOP-CONFIDENCE.md` explaining what is evidenced, decided, gated, unvalidated, and not authorized for engineering.

## What good looks like
A reviewer can tell what is evidenced, decided, conditional, unvalidated, and outside engineering authorization.

## Stop
The review passes and `workshop-output/WORKSHOP-CONFIDENCE.md` exists.

## Recover if needed
If a check fails, return only to the artifact where the defect originated, repair it without inventing evidence, then repeat Step 10.
