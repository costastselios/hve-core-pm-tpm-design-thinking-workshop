# Step 10 — Final Review

**Agent:** None

## Your role in this step
Verify traceability, gates, confidence and boundaries.

## Do
Confirm traceability, outcome/capability hierarchy, excluded scope, gated candidates, complete gate records, no implementation artifacts, and Iteration 2 remains unvalidated. Create `../../workshop-output/WORKSHOP-CONFIDENCE.md` with these headings:

- **Evidenced:** what the supplied research and prototype evidence supports, with its confidence.
- **Decided:** Product Owner choices made without direct evidence.
- **Gated:** open gates, with references to their gate records.
- **Unvalidated:** claims still needing representative-user validation, including the Iteration 2 redesign.
- **First gate to resolve:** which gate you'd resolve first before engineering, and why.

## What good looks like
Reviewer can tell evidenced vs decided vs gated vs unvalidated.

## Stop
Review passes and confidence file exists.

## Recover if needed
Return only to the artifact containing the defect; repair without inventing evidence.
