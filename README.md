# HVE Core for PMs/TPMs: Product Ambiguity to Governed Backlog

A self-paced workshop focused on the **Design Thinking lifecycle**: start with an ambiguous product idea, learn through evidence and prototypes, then carry the result into a governed BRD, PRD, and Epic/Feature planning baseline.

## What you'll learn
- Reduce product ambiguity without inventing user needs.
- Preserve evidence confidence, contradictions, assumptions, and open questions.
- Move from research evidence to a coherent user concept and prototype-learning loop.
- Hand Design Thinking evidence safely into BRD and PRD creation.
- Govern unresolved dependencies as explicit gates.
- Create a traceable Epic/Feature backlog without prematurely entering engineering execution.

## Scope boundary
**HVE Design Thinking Methods 7–9 are outside this workshop. Workshop Steps 7–10 continue after Design Thinking Method 6 with BRD, PRD, Epic/Feature backlog creation, and final review.**

Architecture, Stories/Tasks, estimates, sprint planning, RPI, code, and pull requests are outside scope.

## Evidence integrity
Supplied research summaries and prototype-test findings are case-study evidence, not raw transcripts or newly collected research.

## Privacy
Do not use actual, approximate, illustrative, or ballpark financial amounts.
